What Is the Gospel?

The most important question anyone can ask is this: what does the Bible say about how a person can be right with God? Here is the answer, step by step. Tap any verse to read it.

Who He Is and What He Requires

God

God is the holy and perfect Creator of everything, including you. Because He made us, He has rightful authority over us. And because He is perfectly holy, His standard is perfection.

Genesis 1:1 Matthew 5:48

The Problem

Sin

Every one of us has fallen short of God's standard. Sin is not just the big things; it is any thought, word, or action that misses His perfection. The Bible says we are all guilty, and our sin separates us from God.

Romans 3:23 Isaiah 59:2

The Penalty

Death

Sin carries a just penalty: death, and eternal separation from God. And no amount of good works, church attendance, or effort can pay that debt. We cannot save ourselves.

Romans 6:23 Hebrews 9:27 Titus 3:5

The Payment

What Christ Did

This is the good news. Jesus Christ, God's own Son, lived the perfect life we could not live, died on the cross in our place, and rose again the third day. His death was the full payment for our sin, offered freely to all who will receive it.

John 3:16 Romans 5:8 1 Corinthians 15:3-4

The Decision

Faith

Salvation is received by turning from your sin to God and placing your full trust in Jesus Christ alone. Not in your works, not in church membership, not in being a good person. In Christ alone.

Acts 16:31 Ephesians 2:8-9 Romans 10:13 John 3:36
