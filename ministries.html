Our Ministries

There is a place for every member of your family at Lighthouse Baptist Church.

Children's Ministry

Ages 4 to 12

Kids join a Bible class taught on their level, with games, songs, and other activities.

Wednesday nights are Patch the Pirate, where they learn to sing, memorize parts, and have a daily time with God.

Youth Ministry

Ages 12 to 18

All teens are invited to join us for activities, Bible class, and services geared for their age group.

Bus Ministry

Any age

We offer a transportation ministry for anyone who needs a ride to church. Call the office and we will arrange a pickup.

Call (765) 384-7572

A nursery is provided for children age 2 and younger at every service, and toddler and children's church run during the Sunday morning service.

Come and See for Yourself

The best way to find where your family fits is to visit. Our greeters will help you find the right class or service for everyone.
